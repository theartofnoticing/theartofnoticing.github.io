❤️ Misza

Misza 🌼

On the day I adopted you, I told you that I was changing your life and your name, and you left your paw print on my heart.

From the very beginning, you understood everything. You were the most sensitive, gentle and intelligent dog I had ever known. At the same time, you had a mind of your own and knew very well how to show your boundaries.

You were open to everyone and curious about the world. You loved sticks, plastic bottles and little balls. Your favourite treats were cottage cheese, buttermilk and surimi.

I called you my little dandelion, because your love and your presence healed my sadness and brightened every day.
